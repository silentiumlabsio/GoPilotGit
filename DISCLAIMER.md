# Important Use Disclaimer

GoPilot assists with organization, document review, trip workflow, and driver records.

It does not:
- issue or approve permits
- replace the official permit
- guarantee route legality or clearance
- replace DOT/state requirements
- replace posted signs or current restrictions
- replace carrier/dispatcher instructions
- replace professional judgment

OCR and document extraction can make mistakes.

Always compare extracted information with the official permit and current applicable requirements before travel.
