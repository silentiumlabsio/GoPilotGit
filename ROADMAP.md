# Public Roadmap

Roadmap items are directional and may change.

## Near-term
- Trip Readiness Center
- Permit Revision Center
- richer automatic trip timeline
- active-trip dashboard improvements
- continued Permit Reader review improvements
- data-integrity checks for linked trip records

## Ongoing
- Android quality and compatibility
- OCR and document review quality
- Saved Trip workflow refinement
- route-review organization
- business record integration
- backup and recovery quality

## Later
- iPhone / iOS release
- broader fleet workflow options
- additional driver workflow automation where it can be implemented safely and reliably

GoPilot will continue to treat official permits and current regulatory requirements as the source of truth.
